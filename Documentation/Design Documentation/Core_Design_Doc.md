# SUCH LIFE #

## BASICS ##

### Genre: ###

2D Top Down Survival Crafter/Metroidvania Dungeon Crawler Hybrid

### Audience: ###

New & Existing Gamers, Ages 13+

### Theme: ###

Loose themeing along the lines of Minecraft-like and light fantasy.

### Platforms: ###

PC only at current, potential for future expansion onto consoles.

### Summary: ###

A grid based survival crafter that emphasises clearing procedurally generated dungeons.

The map has 2 distinct areas:

Overworld:

- Starting Hub

- Basic (crafting) resources to loot

- Dungeon entrances

- Basic enemies

Dungeons:

- Procedurally generated

- New resources and loot

- New enemies

- Bosses

- Beating awards item that unlocks new areas of the maps

### Movement: ###

Players can move using traditional WASD movement. 

Players can sprint using shift. Short cool down after sprinting for a time.

Players can roll using r. Short cool down afer rolling.

### Day/Night Cycle: ###

Brighter at day, darker at night.

At night, enemies can spawn outside of dungeons. 

Dungeons are more difficult at night, but grants better rewards.

### Inventory: ###

8 Hot Bar Slots

8 x 5 Inventory Slots

Openable Whenever

Potential to increase size in future.

### Crafting: ###

3 x 3 Grid

Openable Whenever

See Crafting_Design_Doc.md for more.

### Enemies: ###

Fox:

- Attacks deal small amounts of damage to player when it makes contact.

- Visual Design is a fox.

Sploody: 

- Moves towards player when close enough and then explodes.

- Visual Design is a mix of a cat and cartoon bomb.

### Dungeons: ###

Series of procedurally generated rooms.

Dungeon is considered clear once all enemies have been defeated.

Dungeon(s) in an area must be cleared to unlock next area.
