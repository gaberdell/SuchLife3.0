# SUCH LIFE - GAMEPLAY LOOP #

## Starting Hub ##

Players spawn in the starting hub. 

The starting hub contains some of the most basic resources such as sticks, and will help get players acquainted with some of the basic aspects of gameplay including breaking items, combat, traps, crafting, etc.

## Dungeons ##

Dungeons are integral to gameplay loop.

New resources can be acquired as well as access to new zones.

To enter a dungeon players interact with a point that teleports them to the Dungeon. They can interact with a similar looking point at the point they were brought to exit the dungeon and return to the point that teleported them into the dungeon in the first place.

Dungeons are procedurally generated.

Access to new zones in only rewarded upon the full clear (defeating all enemies) of a dungeon.

Dungeons are more challenging at night, but also offer greater rewards.

Enemies can sometimes "leak" out of dungeons and present a challenge to players.

## New Zones ##

Zones are unlocked by full clearing a dungeon.

Each zone is themed and will provide new and sometimes unique items for players to utilize.

New zones also offer new dungeons to unlock further zones.

## Death ##

Upon death players will drop their items and have the option to quit to menu or continue playing, respawning at a designated respawn point.
